# Customer Letter: Turning Lost Margin into Defensible Cash

Dear E-Commerce Seller & Warehouse Operations Leader,

Every month, when your channel settlement report arrives, thousands of dollars have evaporated before reaching your bank account.

There are inbound prep defect penalties on units you know your team bagged and labeled properly. There are elevated fulfillment fee tier bumps on items that never changed dimension. There are lost units in fulfillment centers where Amazon acknowledges inventory shortage but assigns zero compensation, and there are customer refunds issued where the returned item was actually received back at your dock weeks ago.

Today, you face a brutal trade-off:
1. **Ignore the deductions**, accepting a permanent 1% to 3% margin tax.
2. **Hire an agency**, sacrificing 20% to 30% of recovered cash while hoping they don’t spam the channel with unsupported claims that threaten your account health.
3. **Attempt manual claims**, pulling operators off the floor to dig through warehouse receipts, spreadsheets, and photo folders for three hours to recover a $2.50 fee.

We built **REMA (Recovery Manager)** to replace manual guesswork with automated, legally defensible recovery.

REMA is not another CSV spreadsheet filter or hallucinating chatbot. It connects the dots across your entire fulfillment chain:
- When Amazon charges a $2.00 inbound defect fee, REMA pulls the exact workstation photographic audit from Prep Manager, verifies polybag seals, barcode legibility, and FNSKU placement, and proves your compliance.
- When an item is billed at an elevated weight tier, REMA compares the fee against your verified SKU baseline and prep packaging specifications to dispute the mis-tier overcharge.
- When Amazon adjusts inventory for lost or damaged goods, REMA cross-references your dock receiving logs to confirm custody and prepare reimbursement filings.

Crucially, **REMA knows when not to claim**. If an item genuinely failed inspection at your prep station, REMA issues a `NO CLAIM` decision with the supporting proof so your team can fix the root cause. If evidence is missing, degraded, or conflicting, REMA routes the case to a Human Review Queue with a concrete action plan instead of guessing.

We call this **Step 5 of 5: Money Back**. Your physical warehouse operations produce the evidence; REMA turns that evidence into the cash you earned.

Sincerely,
